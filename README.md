# EveryThing / IET

**A modular software platform for connecting real-world planning, business, collaboration, content, marketplace, and financial workflows in one coherent system.**

> This repository is a **public showcase** of the project. The production source code is maintained privately.

## Why this project exists

Many real-world activities are fragmented across separate tools: planning, business records, needs and offers, deals, content, money, collaboration, permissions, and public-facing pages.

EveryThing / IET explores how these can be modeled as connected parts of one system without forcing that internal complexity onto the user.

The guiding product principle is:

> **The Simplicity Is the Satisfaction Key.**

## Selected product areas

### Planner
A time-aware planning system for fixed, flexible, recurring, and long-range activities, with readiness, lifecycle rules, progress, evidence, reminders, and historical evaluation.

### Businesses & marketplace
Business profiles, catalogs, goods/services, needs and offers, customer relationships, public listings, and deal formation.

### Deals & agreements
Work and collaboration flows that can connect people, responsibilities, quantities, payments, progress, and planning.

### Bookkeeping
Personal and business financial recording designed to connect with the broader operational system rather than live as an isolated accounting module.

### Content & collaboration
Public/private content, invitations, sharing, evidence, media, and collaboration-oriented workflows.
